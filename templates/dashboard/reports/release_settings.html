Allowed despite balance
How it works
  • Report cards are generated as drafts. Learners see nothing until you release them (on the run page).
  • When the hold is on, a learner with unpaid fees sees "Withheld – balance UGX ..." instead of the report card. Parents who open the SMS link see the same.
  • The check is live: as soon as the fees are paid (including SchoolPay payments) the report card opens. Nothing needs to be re-generated.
  • To let one learner through (payment plan, bursary), use Allowed despite balance.
  • Staff can always view, download and print every report card.
